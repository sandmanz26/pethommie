# Competitive Landscape: Pet Hotel / Boarding / Pet Care Management Software (Global + Indonesia/SEA)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Many prices come from aggregator listings (Capterra, G2, GetApp, Krowdbase, ToolRadar) or competitor-authored blogs, and they often conflict. Conflicts are flagged inline.

## Global players: core features and pricing (USD/month)

### Takeaway
The US/UK market is mature and consolidating. Togetherwork now owns Gingr, PetExec and Revelation Pets, and PetExec enquiries go to Gingr. Entry prices run from about $25 to $150/month per location. Boarding-focused suites (Gingr, Kennel Connection, PetExec) sit at the top, around $105–155. Grooming-first tools (MoeGo, DaySmart, Time To Pet) start at $25–49, and boarding is often an add-on. Every serious tool includes a lodging/run calendar with capacity control, vaccine enforcement, online booking/client portal, integrated card payments, deposits, SMS/email reminders and report cards.

**Core feature set (Gingr as the boarding reference)**
- Lodging calendar showing occupied/open kennels and runs by day. Automated capacity groups prevent overbooking, and the online portal marks spots unavailable once they fill. — [Gingr kennel software](https://www.gingrapp.com/kennel-software)
- Vaccination uploads can be required before online booking, and reservations are blocked when vaccines are expired or missing. — [Gingr](https://www.gingrapp.com/kennel-software)
- Report cards with notes and photos go out by email or SMS. Other features: PreCheck online check-in with upsells, a pet-parent mobile app, self-registration, deposits at online booking, integrated payments with convenience-fee pass-through, memberships/packages, automated pricing (partial-day to full-stay, multi-pet discounts), and email/SMS reminders. — [Gingr](https://www.gingrapp.com/kennel-software)
- Kennel Connection bundles boarding, daycare, grooming, training and retail modules, plus a pet-parent portal, online booking, SMS and integrated cards. Desktop/hybrid versions suit unreliable internet. — [KennelBooker](https://www.kennelbooker.com/blog/kennel-software-reviews.html)
- Revelation Pets offers an online booker, Stripe-based payments, reminders, vaccine management and free data upload. — [KennelBooker](https://www.kennelbooker.com/blog/kennel-software-reviews.html)

### Inferences
- **Table stakes (global):** reservation/lodging calendar with run/room occupancy and capacity limits; check-in/out; pet + owner profiles with vaccine tracking and enforcement; online booking or a client portal; deposits and integrated payments; automated reminders; basic sales reporting.
- **Differentiators globally:** report cards with photos (Gingr reviewers call this the feature clients "rave about"), a branded pet-parent app, memberships/packages, automated stay pricing rules, multi-location analytics, and offline/hybrid capability.
- Feeding/medication schedules are rarely highlighted on marketing pages (not mentioned on Gingr's kennel page), so they are probably an embedded care-record feature rather than a headline. This is worth confirming.
- Consolidation under Togetherwork, plus review complaints about post-acquisition stagnation and fee increases, shows that incumbents are vulnerable on service. That matters less for Indonesia, where none of them operate locally.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve official Gingr (domain failed) or MoeGo boarding/daycare pricing pages directly, so those figures are aggregator-based.
- I found no verified data on feeding/medication schedule features per vendor.
- I did not verify ProPet or Pawfinity feature details from primary sources.

## Indonesia/SEA players: features, IDR pricing, traction, shutdowns

### Takeaway
Indonesia already has several local, pet-specific SaaS products priced at about Rp90k–888k per outlet per month. They are mostly POS-first: kasir + grooming + hotel + clinic. PAWSI and Enimalbiz are the closest direct competitors for pet hotel management. Generic POS tools (Moka, Pawoon, Olsera, Majoo) are commonly used but do not support boarding well. Vet-clinic SaaS is a separate, crowded niche at about Rp140k–350k/mo. No local product shows public traction numbers or a strong boarding-specific UX (report cards, owner photo updates, feeding/med schedules). None of the global players (Gingr, MoeGo and others) shows Indonesian localization (IDR, QRIS, WhatsApp, Bahasa).

### Cited Findings
**PAWSI (pawsi.id), PT PAWSI Digital Indonesia, the most complete local competitor**
- Boarding: room assignment (e.g., "Suite A-03"), check-in/out scheduling, and stays billed together with meals and retail.
- Grooming: weight-based size tiers that auto-set price and duration, add-ons, packages, and seasonal pricing.
- POS: split payments (e.g., cash + QRIS), deposits, partial payments, loyalty points and an edit audit trail. Online payments run through Xendit or DOKU on Pro Plus.
- Pet profiles have vaccine and medical tabs and a "Report" tab whose contents are not described. Also: inventory (PO, batch, expiry, consignment), staff permissions, customer portal, an AI assistant ("Angel"), and a separate PAWSI Vet package.
- Pricing per branch per month: Home Grooming Rp288,000, Professional Rp388,000, PAWSI Vet Rp488,000, Pro Plus Rp888,000. Billing is prepaid quarterly, or yearly at the price of 10 months, with no VAT. 7-day trial without a card.
- Traction: "Trusted by pet businesses across Indonesia" with 25 logos and 2 testimonials, but no customer count. It is registered as a PSE (2026). WhatsApp is used for support/onboarding only; it is not described as an in-product feature. — [PAWSI](https://pawsi.id/en)
- A PAWSI blog (competitor-authored) says Moka, Pawoon and Olsera handle retail well but "buckle" once grooming, boarding or daycare is added. — [PAWSI blog](https://pawsi.id/en/blog)

**Enimalbiz (enimalbiz.com), Jakarta (Kelapa Gading)**
- Free plan: Rp0 with 1 outlet, 2 staff, 100 SKU, no grooming/hotel. Pro Rp90,000/outlet/mo. Pro Plus Rp250,000 adds grooming, hotel, booking, and cage management (cage status: occupied/empty/needs cleaning, check-in/out). Ultimate Rp350,000 adds medical records with vaccine history and a clinic system. Enterprise (API, private label) is custom.
- Payments are cash, debit and QRIS. Includes an owner dashboard for multi-outlet stock, sales and staff performance, plus an Android app. No in-app WhatsApp feature is described, and no customer count is given (4 testimonials). — [Enimalbiz](https://enimalbiz.com/)

- Tracxn counts 21 "pet care products" startups in Indonesia with only 1 funded. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/pet-care-products-startups-in-indonesia/__XWnRPfirV5UEvsRT-SfmlTdQrCdi2j6gi8UBDT18JXg#top-companies)
- Indonesian startup funding fell 54.3% YoY in H1 2025 (DailySocial report), so the climate for VC-backed pet SaaS is tough. — [DailySocial](https://dailysocial.id/p/local-ai-innovations-indonesias-startup)

### Inferences
- **Local price anchor:** pet-hotel-capable local SaaS costs about Rp250k (Enimalbiz Pro Plus) to Rp388k–888k (PAWSI) per outlet per month, roughly US$15–55. That is 3–10x cheaper than Gingr/Kennel Connection. A new entrant pricing above Rp400k needs a clear boarding-specific advantage.
- Local competitors are built **POS-first** (kasir, stock, grooming tiers). Boarding is a module with room status and check-in/out. None advertises daily report cards/photo updates to owners, feeding/medication schedules, vaccine-gated booking, or WhatsApp-native owner communication. That is the visible white space.
- Generic POS tools (Moka, Olsera, Pawoon, Majoo) are the real incumbent "competitor" for small pet shops, together with WhatsApp + Excel/notebooks.
- None of the Indonesia-listed products discloses user counts, which suggests the market is small and fragmented.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed shutdowns of Indonesian pet SaaS startups. Searches on DailySocial, Tech in Asia and e27 returned nothing specific.
- I found no traction data (number of outlets) for PAWSI, Enimalbiz, AKU MPOS or the vet SaaS products.
- I did not verify Olsera's or Majoo's official pet modules or their pricing from primary sources.
- I did not collect Google Play install counts or ratings for local apps.

## Most praised vs. most complained-about features in reviews

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise ease of use, report cards and integrated booking-to-payment flows. The biggest complaints are support quality (especially after acquisitions), bugs in booking, messaging and payments, inaccurate financial reports, rising or opaque fees, weak mobile apps and split systems (separate payments).

### Cited Findings
- Gingr (Capterra about 4.5–4.6, ~210 reviews): the UI is easy to navigate and learn, and "report card features are what our clients rave about the most". Complaints: "customer service was eliminated, only AI chat bots now", unreturned calls/emails, persistent booking/messaging/payment bugs, inaccurate financial reports, and fee increases after private-equity ownership (2026 reviews). — [Capterra Gingr reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/136469/Gingr/reviews/); [Capterra CA](https://www.capterra.ca/reviews/136469/gingr)
- Kennel Connection complaints: bugs/crashes, learning curve, complicated pricing. PetExec complaints: administrative complexity, few integrations, weaker mobile. Revelation Pets complaints: limited mobile and customization, "not updated since acquisition". DoggieDashboard complaint: no card payments, so bookings and payments are split across systems. — [KennelBooker](https://www.kennelbooker.com/blog/kennel-software-reviews.html)
- MoeGo (Capterra 4.7, 231 reviews): praised for being easy to learn and user-friendly. Complaints: workflows harder than in a competitor, an early-cancellation fee, and support not answering the question asked. — [Capterra MoeGo reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/165732/MoeGo/reviews/); MoeGo is seen as grooming-first, with boarding secondary — [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/045746/moego)
- DaySmart Pet (Capterra ~4.3, 220 reviews): long-term users praise support. Complaints: long phone waits and escalation needed, and the non-cloud base version is frozen. — [Capterra BR DaySmart](https://www.capterra.com.br/software/93413/DaySmart-Pet)
- KennelBooker notes that missing a staff mobile app matters "for staff moving around the facility". — [Happy Pet Tech blog](https://www.happypet.tech/blog/guides/best-pet-boarding-software-in-2026-complete-comparison-guide)

### Inferences
- For an Indonesian product, responsive human support (WhatsApp-based, as PAWSI offers 7 days a week) is a competitive weapon, given that global incumbents lose points here.
- Reliable payments and reports and a good staff mobile experience are must-haves. Report cards with photos are the most-loved owner-facing feature.

### Gaps
- I did not collect App Store or Google Play reviews (global or Indonesian apps). There are no Indonesian user reviews of PAWSI or Enimalbiz beyond vendor testimonials.

## Integration expectations: WhatsApp, payment gateways (Midtrans/Xendit/QRIS), Google Calendar

### Takeaway
Global tools rely on SMS/email (SMS often a paid add-on), and none advertises WhatsApp. In SEA, WhatsApp is the de facto operating channel, and the SEA entrants (Happy Pet Tech, Pettle) position against "WhatsApp chains". Locally, QRIS is already standard (Enimalbiz, PAWSI), and PAWSI integrates Xendit/DOKU for online payments. I found no evidence of Midtrans integrations or Google Calendar sync among local pet SaaS products.

### Cited Findings
- Kennel Connection two-way SMS costs +$50/mo, and MoeGo caps SMS at 200–900 per van/location by tier. — [KennelBooker](https://www.kennelbooker.com/blog/kennel-software-reviews.html); [MoeGo pricing](https://www.moego.pet/pricing)
- Animalo uses SMS and email, with no WhatsApp. Happy Pet Tech claims WhatsApp built in natively. — [Happy Pet Tech blog](https://www.happypet.tech/blog/guides/best-pet-boarding-software-in-2026-complete-comparison-guide)
- Pettle (Singapore) pitches replacing WhatsApp chains, spreadsheets and folders. — [Pettle](https://www.getpettle.com/post/the-pet-boarding-software-built-for-singapore-kennels-and-daycares)
- Malaysian pet hotel G-Pet uses WhatsApp numbers alongside online boarding registration. — [G-Pet](https://g-pet.com/)
- PAWSI: QRIS in split payments, Xendit/DOKU online payments (Pro Plus), WhatsApp for support only. Enimalbiz: cash, debit, QRIS. — [PAWSI](https://pawsi.id/en); [Enimalbiz](https://enimalbiz.com/)
- Pet Manager integrates with Xero accounting, syncing customers, invoices and payments. — [Xero App Store](https://apps.xero.com/id/app/pet-manager)

### Inferences
- For Indonesia, the expected integrations are QRIS (table stakes) and WhatsApp for confirmations, reminders, report cards/photos and deposit links. WhatsApp is a likely differentiator, because no local competitor advertises it in-product. Next come a payment gateway for online deposits (Xendit and DOKU are used by PAWSI; Midtrans is a likely alternative) and possibly accounting export (Jurnal/Accurate, unverified).
- Google Calendar sync appears to be a low priority. No competitor highlighted it.

### Gaps
- I did not verify Midtrans usage by any pet SaaS, WhatsApp Business API costs in Indonesia, or Indonesian SMB WhatsApp adoption statistics in this session.
- I found no source on Google Calendar integration demand for pet hotels.
