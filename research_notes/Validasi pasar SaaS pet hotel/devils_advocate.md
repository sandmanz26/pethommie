# Devil's Advocate: The Case AGAINST a Vertical B2B SaaS for Pet Hotels in Indonesia

Scope: the strongest counter-evidence a skeptical investor would raise, Indonesia focus, 2020-2026. Every claim is labeled [EVIDENCE] (sourced) or [SPECULATION] (my inference or an illustrative assumption). Research limits: about 15 searches and fetches. Several key numbers, including the national count of pet hotels and pet-business software adoption, do not exist in public sources. That absence is itself a finding: nobody has sized this market publicly.

## Q1. Failures, shutdowns and pivots of Indonesian SMB SaaS and pet startups, and why

### Takeaway
The best-funded Indonesian micro-SMB software play, Lummo/BukuKas (about US$80M Series C in 2022), went into voluntary liquidation in 2023 and returned cash to investors. Pet platforms in the region have wound down or gone quiet citing funding difficulty. Public postmortems rarely state the cause directly. The pattern is easy to see anyway: free or cheap SMB tools reached many users but did not monetize, and the attempted pivots (e-commerce storefronts) failed too.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE] Lummo (formerly BukuKas) entered voluntary liquidation according to Singapore ACRA filings. — [DealStreetAsia](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/indonesia-lummo-liquidation-360554)
- [EVIDENCE] Before liquidating, Lummo was reported to be weighing a shutdown and planning to return more than US$70M to investors, so it closed with most of its capital still unspent. It had not found a business worth spending that capital on. — [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/lummo-may-close-operations-340722)
- [EVIDENCE] Lummo was founded in 2019 as a digital bookkeeping app for micro and small businesses. It added LummoSHOP (e-commerce), raised about US$80M in its January 2022 Series C, and entered voluntary liquidation in September 2023 with Kroll as liquidator. — [Descubre.vc profile](https://www.descubre.vc/bukukas) (secondary profile)
- [EVIDENCE] Investors in that Series C included Jeff Bezos, Tiger Global and Sequoia. The company later cut about half its India workforce while shifting focus away from LummoShop. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/sequoia-capital-backed-lummo-lays-off-50-indian-workforce/)
- [EVIDENCE] The product bundled transaction logging, fee-free money transfers, reports and inventory. Free payments meant it gave away the one feature it could most easily have charged for. — [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/organization/bukukas)
- [EVIDENCE] The Indian equivalent tells the same story: Khatabook shut its MyStore e-commerce product and reportedly had no operating revenue through FY20 despite heavy spending. OKCredit's similar product also struggled. — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/?p=114075)
- [EVIDENCE] Mekari, Indonesia's largest SME SaaS company, laid off about 5% of staff (around 70 people) to "prioritise high-impact initiatives". Even the category leader is cutting. — [DealStreetAsia](https://dealstreetasia.com/stories/mekari-layoffs-424034)
- [EVIDENCE] DealStreetAsia describes an "Indonesian startup shakeout": fewer shutdowns in 2024, but more layoffs as companies put survival first. — [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/indonesian-startup-shakeout-387425/)
- [EVIDENCE] A legal analysis counts at least 102 Indonesian startups permanently closed. In its sample, SaaS had a lower failure share (8.99%) than travel (21.62%). This **cuts the other way**: SaaS as a category fails less often than consumer marketplaces. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/corporate-and-company-law/1314686/failed-start-uptech-companies-in-southeast-asia-why-they-failed-and-effective-solutions-from-an-indonesian-law-perspective)
- [EVIDENCE] My Pet World, a regional pet platform that combined shopping, services, social features and pet profiles, wound down. Its founder blamed a failed regional fundraise and the funding climate, after reaching 10,000+ registered users and 2,000+ pet profiles. The post is undated. — [Founder LinkedIn](https://ae.linkedin.com/in/anameekagupta)
- [EVIDENCE] CB Insights lists Pawshake, the pet-sitting marketplace active in Singapore, as "Seed | Dead" with about US$1.55M raised. The site still appears live, so this status is unverified and may be stale. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/pawshake); [Pawshake SG](https://www.pawshake.com.sg/about-us)
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesian pet platforms such as Petskita (2020) and Pompy Go (an incubator project) appear in press and academic reports, but I found no evidence of funding rounds or scale. — [Kontan](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/bisnis-hewan-peliharaan-booming-startup-petskita-tangkap-peluang-pet-economy); [UMN repository](https://kc.umn.ac.id/id/eprint/45990/)
- [EVIDENCE] In 2017, Indonesian customer-service SaaS Bornevia shut down and told users to migrate to Zendesk. This is an early sign that local SMB SaaS gets squeezed by global horizontal tools. — [e27](https://e27.co/indonesian-customer-service-platform-bornevia-to-shut-down-in-april-20170316/)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] The Lummo lesson for this idea: reaching many micro-merchants in Indonesia is easy when the product is free, and getting them to pay is the hard part. A pet-hotel SaaS starts from a far smaller base than Lummo's millions of warungs, so it cannot even take the "free users, monetize later" route Lummo tried.
- [SPECULATION] Pet consumer platforms in the region have failed or stalled at the funding stage, before reaching scale. Over 2020-2026 I found no evidence that VCs fund Indonesian pet-tech at scale.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian-language postmortem quoting Lummo's founders on the reason for closure was found (Tech in Asia and DailySocial pieces may exist behind a paywall).
- Qasir's and Moka's current status and churn were not verified.
- No confirmed Indonesian pet-SaaS shutdown was found, possibly because almost nobody has tried to build one.

## Q2. How many pet hotels could realistically pay Rp100k-500k/month? SAM/SOM math

### Takeaway
No official count of pet hotels or pet shops in Indonesia exists in BPS or industry public data. Reported pet-hotel prices are Rp30-45k per animal per night, and normal volumes at small operators are about 10-20 animals per day. At those levels, Rp100-500k/month is 3-15 room-nights, a noticeable share of a small operator's margin. Under explicit assumptions, the paying market looks like low thousands of outlets and an SOM of a few billion rupiah ARR, which is not venture scale.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesia has no national count of pet shops or pet hotels in public sources. Euromonitor calls the pet-shop channel "highly fragmented". Its example, Pet Station, has only 5 outlets in Jabodetabek. — [Euromonitor via ResearchPool](https://app.researchpool.com/provider/euromonitor/pet-station-in-pet-care-indonesia)
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesia's pet care market is estimated at about US$605M in 2025, rising to about US$700M by 2030 (Vyansa). That is slow growth, and other estimates differ widely. — cited via search summary of Vyansa Intelligence; original not fetched (treat as weak)
- [EVIDENCE] Cats dominate: Indonesia had the highest cat-ownership rate of 11 APAC markets at 47% (Rakuten 2021). In 2018, ownership was 37% for cats vs 16% for dogs. — [Databoks/Katadata](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/economics-macro/statistics/a9c5ec05e64e14b/cats-are-the-most-popular-pets-in-indonesia-according-to-rakuten-insight); [Rakuten Insight](https://insight.rakuten.com/pet-market-in-asia/)
- [EVIDENCE] Malang cat boarding (March 2025): normally 15-20 cats/day, rising to 140/day (capacity) before Lebaran. Price is Rp30,000 per cat, up to Rp90,000 for a 5-cat cage. Grooming is Rp50-60k. — [Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/bzGCRXJZ-mudik-lebaran-tempat-penitipan-kucing-di-malang-laris-manis)
- [EVIDENCE] Pati pet hotel: normally about 10 animals, 30+ before Lebaran. — [Detik Jateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/jawa-tengah-meriah/d-7847427/libur-lebaran-penitipan-peliharaan-di-pati-laris-manis)
- [EVIDENCE] Petshop Indonesia chain (2022): boarding Rp35,000/day for cats and Rp45,000/day for dogs (without food). Demand is about 3x normal before Lebaran, with about 50 animals/day at one branch. — [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/economics/jelang-lebaran-jasa-penitipan-hewan-meningkat-tiga-kali-lipat/all)
- [EVIDENCE] The 2021 chairman of the Indonesian startup association Atsindo named affordable pricing and simple features as the two main barriers for SaaS selling to UMKM. — [Bisnis.com](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20210506/266/1391002/saas-perlu-tuntaskan-hal-ini-untuk-dongkrak-pengguna)

### Inferences: illustrative SAM/SOM math (all assumptions are [SPECULATION])
- **Unit revenue of a typical small pet hotel:** 15 animals/night x Rp35k x 30 nights is about **Rp15.75M/month** in boarding revenue off-peak (based on the Malang/Pati/Petshop Indonesia figures above). Rp300k/month of SaaS is about **1.9% of boarding revenue**, roughly 9 room-nights. For the cheapest operators (around 10 animals at Rp30k, about Rp9M/month), Rp300k is about 3.3%. That is a meaningful line item for owner-operators who already run on WhatsApp.
- **Outlet count:** with no census, I assume [SPECULATION] about 3,000-8,000 businesses offering boarding (pet shops, clinics and standalone hotels), concentrated in Jabodetabek, Surabaya, Bandung, Medan, Bali and Semarang. A rough method would be to count Google Maps listings for "pet hotel" / "penitipan hewan" per city. This needs validation and may be badly off in either direction.
- **SAM (businesses with 10+ boarding units, digitally minded, in tier-1/2 cities):** assume 30-40% of those, about **1,000-3,000 outlets**.
- **SOM (realistic share in years 3-5):** 10-20% of SAM, about **100-600 paying outlets**. At Rp250k ARPU that is about **Rp0.3-1.8B ARR (around US$20k-110k)**. Even if the whole SAM paid Rp500k, the ceiling is about Rp18B ARR (around US$1.1M). This is a bootstrapped lifestyle business, not a venture outcome.
- [SPECULATION] Expanding to grooming salons, clinics and pet shops (a "pet services OS") raises the outlet count several-fold. At that point, though, you compete head-on with vet PIMS and POS vendors.

### Gaps
- No primary data exists on the number of pet hotels, their revenue distribution, or current software spend. **Action:** scrape Google Maps / Instagram counts per city and run 20-30 owner interviews with a price-sensitivity question.
- I found no Indonesian pet-owner spending-per-month data that would confirm boarding frequency (the Jakpat survey exists, but its figures were not retrieved). — [Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/?p=20084)

## Q3. Are generic tools already "good enough"?

### Takeaway
Probably yes for most small operators. A pet hotel's core needs are a booking calendar, a customer and pet profile, payment, and a daily photo update to the owner. WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Sheets/Calendar cover these at zero marginal cost. Generic POS tools (Majoo Rp249k+/outlet/month, Moka about Rp299k+) are already pitched at pet shops and sit at the top of the proposed price band. Global vertical tools (Fresha, MoeGo, Gingr, GoPet AI, PawCare) already offer pet grooming and boarding features, but I found no confirmed Indonesian adoption.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE] Majoo's official pricing: Starter Rp249,000, Advance Rp499,000 and Prime Rp999,000 per outlet per month, with a 12-month minimum. A pet SaaS priced at Rp100-500k lands right in POS territory, and owners may already pay for a POS. — [Majoo FAQ](https://majoo.id/faq)
- [EVIDENCE] Moka POS reportedly starts at about Rp299,000/month per branch with a 14-day trial (third-party source, unverified). HitPay also markets free POS in Indonesia. — [HitPay](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/pos-system-indonesia-comparison); [HitPay free POS](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/sistem-aplikasi-pos-gratis-di-indonesia)
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesian POS and ERP vendors (Accurate POS, HashMicro) already publish "aplikasi kasir pet shop" content and position themselves for pet shops: sales, stock, multi-branch, loyalty. — [Accurate](https://accurate.id/aplikasi-kasir/aplikasi-kasir-pet-shop/); [HashMicro](https://www.hashmicro.com/id/blog/kasir-petshop/)
- [EVIDENCE] Fresha markets a pet-grooming product (appointments, pet history, reminders, deposits, daily payouts). Its plans are about US$14.95-19.95/month, plus a 20% marketplace commission on new clients (minimum US$6) according to a competitor's blog. — [Fresha pet grooming](https://www.fresha.com/for-business/pet-grooming); [Franpos (competitor, biased)](https://franpos.com/grooming-salon-software-online-booking-and-payments)
- [EVIDENCE] Pet-specific global tools exist: MoeGo, Gingr, Pet Manager, EasyWeek, Groomer.io, GoPet AI (booking, pet profiles, invoicing, check-in/out for salons, boarding and pet shops), and PawCare. — [Franpos](https://franpos.com/grooming-salon-software-online-booking-and-payments); [WebCatalog GoPet AI](https://webcatalog.io/en/apps/gopet-ai); [WebCatalog PawCare](https://webcatalog.io/id/apps/pawcare)
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesian university theses build custom web grooming/booking apps for individual pet shops (e.g., Royal Petshop Medan). Demand for digitization exists, but it is met by one-off custom builds. — [Telkom University](https://repositori.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/files/171910/bab1/aplikasi-pelayanan-jasa-grooming-berbasis-web-di-royal-petshop-medan.pdf)
- [EVIDENCE] None of the pet-hotel news reports reviewed (Malang, Pati, Petshop Indonesia chain) mention any software or app. Booking is described as "reserve in advance" with staff checking availability by hand. — [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/economics/jelang-lebaran-jasa-penitipan-hewan-meningkat-tiga-kali-lipat/all); [Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/bzGCRXJZ-mudik-lebaran-tempat-penitipan-kucing-di-malang-laris-manis)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] The real competitor is "WhatsApp + notebook/Sheets + QRIS", which is free, familiar and already integrated with how customers talk to the business. For a 10-20-cage operator, the pain is real for about 2-4 weeks a year (Lebaran, Christmas/New Year) and mild otherwise.
- [SPECULATION] Larger chains (Petshop Indonesia, Pet Station, clinic-hospitals like RSH UNAIR) have the most need, but they are likelier to use POS, ERP or vet PIMS, or to build in-house, and they have longer sales cycles.
- [SPECULATION] Fresha's commission and marketplace model, and its booking UX built for time-slot salons, fit multi-night boarding poorly. That leaves a gap, but it does not prove anyone will pay to fill it.

### Gaps
- No survey data on Indonesian pet businesses' actual software usage (Fresha, Booksy, SimplyBook.me, Majoo, Notion). Primary research is needed.

## Q4. Seasonality: does a pet hotel need software all year?

### Takeaway
Demand spikes 3-7x around Lebaran (and likely at school holidays and year-end), then falls back to a level a notebook can handle. This creates the classic seasonal-SaaS churn problem: owners sign up when they are in pain and cancel afterward.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE] Malang: about 5x the normal level before Lebaran (15-20/day up to 140/day). — [Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/bzGCRXJZ-mudik-lebaran-tempat-penitipan-kucing-di-malang-laris-manis)
- [EVIDENCE] Pati: about 10 to 30+ animals, about 3x. — [Detik Jateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/jawa-tengah-meriah/d-7847427/libur-lebaran-penitipan-peliharaan-di-pati-laris-manis)
- [EVIDENCE] Petshop Indonesia chain: about 3x normal boarding demand before Lebaran, with some branches fully booked. — [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/economics/jelang-lebaran-jasa-penitipan-hewan-meningkat-tiga-kali-lipat/all)
- [EVIDENCE] Kediri (2026) and RSH UNAIR Surabaya (2026): bookings almost full or quota reached around Lebaran. — [Metro TV News Kediri](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NrWC88DJ-jasa-penitipan-hewan-di-kediri-ramai-dipesan-pemudik-jelang-lebaran); [UNAIR](https://unair.ac.id/rsh-unair-berkomitmen-tetap-melayani-24-jam-dikala-libur-lebaran-2026/)
- [EVIDENCE] General SMB SaaS churn benchmarks: about 3-7% monthly is commonly cited, and two cohort-style studies put it at 6.2-6.4%. Micro-businesses (fewer than 10 employees) churn about 8.9% monthly. These are vendor and agency sources, not Indonesia-specific. — [Kalungi](https://kalungi.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks); [Visionary Marketing](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/saas-churn-benchmarks-2026); [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/?p=6073)

### Inferences
- [SPECULATION] At 8.9% monthly churn (the micro-business benchmark), about 67% of a cohort is gone within 12 months. Indonesian micro-operators are likely worse given price sensitivity and the seasonal pain pattern. Average customer lifetime would be about 11 months. At Rp250k ARPU, LTV is about Rp2.8M gross, or about Rp2.2M at 80% gross margin.
- [SPECULATION] **CAC vs LTV:** field sales or onboarding in Indonesia typically involves visits, demos and WhatsApp hand-holding. A CAC of Rp1-3M per paying outlet is plausible but not sourced. That gives an LTV/CAC of about 1x or worse, versus the healthy target of 3x or more. Self-serve acquisition via Instagram/TikTok could be cheaper but is unproven for B2B in this niche.
- [SPECULATION] Seasonal pricing (pay only in peak months, or per-booking fees) aligns with value, but it caps ARPU further.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian SMB SaaS churn data was found (no public numbers from Majoo, Moka or Mekari). The CAC figure is an assumption.
- The size of the Natal/New Year and school-holiday peaks is unquantified.

## Q5. Alternative models, and what would need to be true for this to work

### Takeaway
The investor-grade counter-thesis is: do not sell subscription software to pet hotels. Either (a) own demand (a B2C booking marketplace for pet hotels, which monetizes the Lebaran scarcity via take-rate or deposits), or (b) go payments-led (free software with QRIS, deposits and payouts, monetized on transaction fees), or (c) widen to a pet-services OS (boarding, grooming, clinic, retail). Each has its own red flags.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE] a16z: adding fintech can raise revenue per customer 2-5x, and vertical markets tend to consolidate around one dominant software provider. — [a16z "Fintech Scales Vertical SaaS"](https://a16z.com/fintech-scales-vertical-saas/)
- [EVIDENCE] Vertical SaaS with embedded fintech reportedly earns 33-60% valuation premiums. SaaS with embedded payments captured 36% of SME acquiring revenue in 2024. — [SaaSRise](https://www.saasrise.com/blog/vertical-saas-ma-vc-report-2026) (secondary)
- [EVIDENCE] At Toast, payments revenue far exceeds subscription revenue. — [Apideck](https://www.apideck.com/blog/embedded-finance-vertical-saas.md)
- [EVIDENCE] Peak-season scarcity is real: consumer guidance tells owners to book well in advance because many pet hotels are full before Lebaran. — [CNA Indonesia](https://www.cna.id/lifestyle/tips-ajak-mudik-anabul-titip-pet-hotel-pet-sitter-liburan-30396)
- [EVIDENCE] Indonesian operators already bundle services. Petshop Indonesia grew into clinics and pet hotels, and Pet Station combines grooming, a pet hotel and a pet pool. — [Fortune IDN](https://www.fortuneidn.com/business/dari-modal-dengkul-ke-40-gerai-petshop-indonesia-bidik-pet-mall-00-4vfn9-7wx4dx); [Euromonitor](https://app.researchpool.com/provider/euromonitor/pet-station-in-pet-care-indonesia)

### Inferences (all [SPECULATION])
- **Why payments-led is weak here too:** the numbers are tiny. A 1% take on about Rp15M/month of GMV per outlet is about Rp150k/month, no better than a subscription. QRIS MDR in Indonesia is regulated and low (0.3% for micro merchants under Bank Indonesia rules; not re-verified in this research), so payments margin is thin. The a16z 2-5x uplift assumes US-style card interchange.
- **Why a marketplace is risky:** disintermediation. Once an owner finds a trusted hotel, they rebook over WhatsApp. Demand concentrates in about 2-4 weeks a year, and failed SEA pet marketplaces (My Pet World; Pawshake possibly dead) show how hard funding and liquidity are.
- **Bundle (pet services OS):** the strongest version of the idea. It raises outlet count and usage frequency (grooming is recurring every 4-8 weeks, unlike boarding) and spreads seasonality. But it puts you up against POS (Majoo/Moka) and vet PIMS.
- **What would need to be true:**
  1. At least 1,000-3,000 outlets with enough volume (≥20 animals/day) for coordination pain to exceed WhatsApp's capacity. Validate with a Google Maps count plus interviews.
  2. A wedge that WhatsApp cannot do and that drives revenue, not just "organization". Candidates: (a) **deposit/DP collection via QRIS** to cut Lebaran no-shows, (b) **automatic daily photo/video report to owners**, a trust feature that justifies premium pricing, (c) **vaccination/health record check at check-in** to cut liability, (d) **peak-season waitlist and capacity calendar**, (e) **a public booking page** (link-in-bio for Instagram/TikTok).
  3. Near-zero CAC through community or owner networks (e.g., Instagram pet-business communities, Petshop chain partnerships, distribution via a POS or pet-food distributor).
  4. Monthly churn below about 3%, possibly via annual prepay discounts before Lebaran.
  5. A path to a second revenue line (payments, B2C demand, supplies or insurance) so ARPU is not capped at Rp100-500k.
- **Strongest wedge, my judgment [SPECULATION]:** "Lebaran-proof booking". That means DP collection, a capacity calendar, auto-updates to owners, and a booking link, sold as a seasonal pack at about Rp199-499k per peak season, before trying for a monthly subscription. It tests willingness to pay where pain is highest.

### Gaps
- No Indonesian data on pet-hotel no-show rates, DP practices, or how much owners would pay for daily photo updates.
- Current QRIS MDR rates for micro merchants were not re-verified in this research.
- I found no Indonesian B2C pet-hotel aggregator with public traction data. Peto appears in CB Insights, but its status is unverified. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/petbacker-vs-peto)
