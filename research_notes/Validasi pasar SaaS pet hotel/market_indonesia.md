# Indonesian Pet Hotel / Pet Boarding Market (Demand + Supply) — Context for a B2B SaaS

Research date: 13 Oct 2026. Labels used: **[HARD]** = reported figure from a named survey or statistical source. **[EST]** = market-research projection, industry claim or vendor estimate. **[ANEC]** = a single business's figures as reported in the press. Older data (before 2022) is flagged.

## 1. Pet owners / pets (cats vs dogs), growth trends, and pet care market size (2023-2026)

### Takeaway
Indonesia is a cat-first market. About 47% of surveyed respondents own a cat and 10% own a dog (Rakuten, 2021), and cat numbers reportedly roughly doubled between 2019 and 2023. Pet care market-size estimates disagree badly, from about US$0.6B to US$2.5B, so no single figure is credible. The firmest number is Euromonitor's IDR 7.8T (2023, +9.8% YoY), which appears to cover pet food. Euromonitor describes 2024-2025 as a soft period of modest growth, as purchasing power weakened even while the number of owners kept rising.

### Cited Findings
**Ownership and population**
- [HARD, but data is from Jan 2021] Rakuten Insight survey: 97,000 respondents across 12 Asian markets. Indonesia had 72% pet ownership among respondents, 47% owned cats and 10% owned dogs. Indonesia had the highest cat-owner share of the 12 markets, and Indonesia and Malaysia were the only markets where cats beat dogs. — [Rakuten Insight, Pet ownership in Asia](https://insight.rakuten.com/pet-ownership-in-asia/); [Katadata Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/economics-macro/statistics/a9c5ec05e64e14b/cats-are-the-most-popular-pets-in-indonesia-according-to-rakuten-insight)
- The 2021 Rakuten figure was still the one cited in July 2026, and I found no newer Rakuten wave. — [RRI Voice of Indonesia](https://rri.co.id/en/lifestyle/2614969/cats-remain-indonesias-most-popular-pets)
- [HARD, but data is from 2018] Rakuten June 2018 wave (4,200 Indonesian respondents): 37% of pet owners had a cat and 15.5% had a dog. This uses a different base from the 2021 survey, so the two are not comparable. — [Statista summary](https://fr.statista.com/statistics/974157/pet-owners-by-type-of-pet-indonesia/)
- [EST] Cat population "surged 98% since 2019 to 5.1 million in 2023". The article does not attribute this figure clearly to a source. — [ICE / Kontan, Dec 2025](https://www.ice.it/it/news/notizie-dal-mondo/297388)
- [HARD, narrow scope] BPS (2023) counts 6.84M cats, but only those kept by agricultural business units. East Java has 2.24M and DIY 1.68M. This is not a count of household pets. — [GoodStats](https://goodstats.id/article/jumlah-kucing-di-diy-tembus-1-6-juta-setara-gabungan-penduduk-bantul-dan-kulon-progo-wr1Lk)
- [EST] Euromonitor (cited via Kompas.id, about 2023): 4.80M owned cats, excluding semi-outdoor and outdoor cats. Another source quotes Royal Canin's market research at 21.6M pet cats and 3.3M pet dogs, year unclear. Sources for national pet counts disagree widely. — [Search summary of Kompas.id/Kontan coverage; Kontan article](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/perusahaan-pet-food-royal-canin-optimistis-tumbuh-15-tahun-ini) (paywalled/403, so the figures could not be verified directly)
- [EST] OCBC Ventura (Aug 2024) repeats the claim that about 72% of households have at least one pet. This traces back to the Rakuten respondent figure, not to a household census. — [OCBC NISP Ventura](https://ocbcnispventura.substack.com/p/the-paw-sitive-trend-of-furry-friends)

**Market size**
- [HARD-ish] Euromonitor: IDR 7.8 trillion in 2023, +9.8% YoY. The context is pet food and feed. — [ICE / Kontan, Dec 2025](https://www.ice.it/it/news/notizie-dal-mondo/297388)
- [EST] Projections compiled by Fortune IDN (Sep 2026):
  - Vyansa Intelligence: US$605M (2025) to US$700M (2030), 2.46% CAGR.
  - Business Software Help: US$2.5B (2025) to US$4.45B (2032), 8.59% CAGR.
  - Jakarta Pet Expo: US$2.30B (2023) to more than US$5.88B (2033), 9.5% CAGR.

  — [Fortune IDN](https://www.fortuneidn.com/business/dari-modal-dengkul-ke-40-gerai-petshop-indonesia-bidik-pet-mall-00-4vfn9-7wx4dx)
- [EST] Mordor Intelligence (cited by listed company MDLA): about US$1.83B, or about Rp 30T, by 2031. — [Investor Trust](https://investortrust.id/business/95378/mdla-perkuat-ekspansi-bisnis-pet-care-bidik-potensi-pasar-rp-30-triliun); [Republika, 24 Feb 2026](https://ekonomi.republika.co.id/berita/taybly522/mdla-bidik-pasar-pet-care-yang-diproyeksi-tembus-183-miliar-dolar-as)
- [HARD, qualitative] Euromonitor "Pet Care in Indonesia" (June 2025): 2024 was "subdued", and 2025 faces economic uncertainty. Weaker purchasing power means only modest value growth, partly offset by an expanding base of urban pet owners. Growth in cat food is expected to come from Gen-Z adoption and more feeding of strays. — [GII listing of the Euromonitor report](https://www.gii.tw/report/eo1761607-pet-care-indonesia.html)
- [HARD, regional] Euromonitor (Nov 2025): globally, cat food was the fastest-growing category from 2020 to 2025 (6% CAGR, versus 3.8% for dog food). Rules on dog ownership and religious preference in APAC are pushing people toward cats. — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251113457439/en/Pet-Care-Market-Experiences-Shift-as-Feline-Favouritism-Drives-Growth-Euromonitor-International)

### Inferences
- A pet hotel SaaS should be designed cat-first: cat boarding, multi-cat cages, and cat vaccination checks. Dog boarding is a secondary, more premium niche concentrated in Jakarta and Bali.
- Most "market size" figures cover food. Services such as boarding, grooming and vet care are not broken out in any public estimate, so a services TAM has to be built bottom-up.
- Spending per owner is under pressure (Euromonitor 2024-25), while the number of owners is growing. This favors low-priced SaaS for small operators.

### Gaps
- No post-2021 survey of owner penetration and no official national pet census. BPS covers only cats kept by agricultural units.
- No public figure for pet services (boarding or grooming) in IDR. The full Euromonitor tables (US$2,450) were not accessible.
- No reliable national dog population figure.

## 2. Number and location of pet hotels, pet shops with boarding, groomers, and vet clinics with boarding

### Takeaway
I found no official count or association registry of pet hotels or pet shops in Indonesia. Supply is fragmented across many single-outlet operators, with only a few small chains (for example PetShop Indonesia with 40 outlets). The supply is visible in Jabodetabek, Bandung, Surabaya/Malang, Semarang/Solo, Yogyakarta and Bali, and also in tier-2/3 towns such as Pati and Karawang.

### Cited Findings
- I found no national count of pet shops, groomers or clinics. Possible sources are OSS/KBLI licensing data, PDHI (the vets' association) or the Ministry of Agriculture (Ditjen PKH), but none were found publicly. — [search result summary; no source located]
- [ANEC] OCBC Ventura: grooming salons and pet hotels mostly run as single- or dual-outlet businesses. — [OCBC NISP Ventura, Aug 2024](https://ocbcnispventura.substack.com/p/the-paw-sitive-trend-of-furry-friends)
- [ANEC] PetShop Indonesia (Depok, founded 2011) has 40 outlets: 30 owned and 10 franchised, all on leased sites. It offers grooming, a pet hotel (seasonal demand) and a clinic. Services are its largest revenue line. It reports about 30% YoY growth and a 30-35% blended gross margin. — [Fortune IDN, Sep 2026](https://www.fortuneidn.com/business/dari-modal-dengkul-ke-40-gerai-petshop-indonesia-bidik-pet-mall-00-4vfn9-7wx4dx)
- [ANEC, 2019] Petloka launched its pet hotel search with 40 verified partners across Jabodetabek, Surabaya, Yogyakarta and Bali. This shows where professional supply was concentrated at the time. — [detikInet, 22 May 2019](https://inet.detik.com/cyberlife/d-4559318/aplikasi-ini-bisa-bantu-cari-hotel-untuk-hewan-peliharaan)
- [ANEC] Lebaran 2025 news coverage features pet hotels in Bandung, Malang, Jakarta, Solo, Karawang and Pati. — [detik Bandung](https://finance.detik.com/foto-bisnis/d-7843323/jasa-penitipan-kucing-di-bandung-meningkat-jelang-lebaran); [Metro TV Malang](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/bzGCRXJZ-mudik-lebaran-tempat-penitipan-kucing-di-malang-laris-manis); [detik Pati](https://www.detik.com/jateng/jawa-tengah-meriah/d-7847427/libur-lebaran-penitipan-peliharaan-di-pati-laris-manis); [kumparan Karawang](https://kumparan.com/kumparannews/mengintip-hotel-kucing-di-karawang-yang-banjir-pesanan-saat-mudik-lebaran-24lc9DOgHel)
- [ANEC] Grooming is often done at vet clinics or at pet shops that work with vets, which suggests the pet shop + groom + boarding + clinic bundle is common. — [Jurnal UM Tapsel](https://jurnal.um-tapsel.ac.id/index.php/nusantara/article/view/2933)

### Inferences
- The likely shape of supply is several thousand micro-operators: home-based cat hotels, pet shops with 10-50 cages, and clinics with boarding. A small premium tier sits in Jakarta and Bali. This is an estimate, not data. Validate it by scraping Google Maps for "pet hotel", "penitipan kucing" and "penitipan hewan" in each city.
- Lebaran coverage reaching tier-2/3 towns (Pati, Karawang, Malang) suggests demand is not limited to Jabodetabek.

### Gaps
- No Google Maps listing counts could be gathered with the tools available. This needs manual or API scraping (Places API or Apify) per city.
- No association for pet hotels or pet shops was found.

## 3. Typical business profile: size, staff, ownership, prices (IDR), peak seasons

### Takeaway
A typical cat hotel has about 12-60 cages and is owner-operated. Cat boarding costs about Rp 30-85K per night in tier-2 cities and Rp 100-140K in Jakarta. Dog boarding in Jakarta costs about Rp 75-180K depending on size. Lebaran (mudik) is the defining peak: bookings double and cages fill completely, in one case occupancy rose about 7-9x. Natal-Tahun Baru is a second peak, especially for dog boarding.

**Natal-Tahun Baru**
- [OLD, 2017] A dog boarder in Kelapa Gading received Christmas bookings from October and was full through December "every year-end". It requires a minimum stay of 2 nights. — [detikFinance 2017](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-3757114/mengintip-peluang-bisnis-penitipan-anjing)

**Ownership model**
- Mostly single- or dual-outlet operators. — [OCBC Ventura](https://ocbcnispventura.substack.com/p/the-paw-sitive-trend-of-furry-friends)
- At PetShop Indonesia, demand for the pet hotel is described as "seasonal". — [Fortune IDN](https://www.fortuneidn.com/business/dari-modal-dengkul-ke-40-gerai-petshop-indonesia-bidik-pet-mall-00-4vfn9-7wx4dx)

### Inferences
- Revenue is very seasonal. A 140-cage operator moving from about 15-20 to 140 cats/day implies off-peak occupancy of roughly 10-15% and 100% at Lebaran. The SaaS value is strongest in peak capacity management: waitlists, deposits, H-x booking cut-offs, check-in/out rush, and vaccination verification. Off-peak, the value lies in grooming add-ons and retention.
- Indicative revenue: a 50-cage cat hotel at about Rp 60K per night, full for about 14 days over Lebaran, earns about Rp 42M in that peak. This is my own calculation, illustrative only.
- Willingness to pay for SaaS is likely low (micro-operators with thin margins). Pricing probably needs to sit in the Rp 50-200K/month range or depend on usage. This is an inference to be validated in interviews.
- Typical staff count was not reported. My inference is 1-5 people (the owner plus helpers).

### Gaps
- No systematic data on staff counts, number of outlets, or the share of owner-operated businesses versus chains.
- No 2023-2026 data on Natal-Tahun Baru demand. The only source is from 2017.
- No data on whether prices rise during peak periods. The sources show flat rates at Lebaran.

## 4. Current booking and operations tools (WhatsApp, IG, spreadsheets, notebooks, POS)

### Takeaway
Booking and owner updates run largely on WhatsApp and Instagram DM, with manual record-keeping (notebook or spreadsheet). Academic studies repeatedly cite this as a pain point: operators struggle to send status updates to many owners, and manual records cause financial and trust losses. Generic POS products (Majoo, Moka, Olsera, HashMicro) handle retail and grooming sales but are not built for boarding. I found no evidence of Indonesian boarding-specific software in use.

### Cited Findings
- UMN thesis (Paws Inn): communication between owners and pet hotels is "masih menggunakan metode manual seperti WhatsApp" (still done manually, for example over WhatsApp), and monitoring is limited. Pet hotels are overwhelmed sending condition updates to many owners via WhatsApp and Instagram. — [UMN repository](https://kc.umn.ac.id/id/eprint/34415/); [BAB I](https://kc.umn.ac.id/id/eprint/34415/2/BAB_I.pdf)
- [OLD, 2018] Juanda Petshop & Clinic, Depok: manual record-keeping "terkadang menyebabkan kerugian baik dalam hal materi maupun kepercayaan pelanggan" (sometimes causes losses of both money and customer trust). — [Jurnal STRING (Unindra)](https://journal.lppmunindra.ac.id/index.php/STRING/article/view/2435)
- UMK thesis: grooming and cat-hotel reservation transactions and records are done manually, so the thesis proposes a booking website. — [UMK eprint](https://eprints.umk.ac.id/22041/2/Bab%201%20TA.pdf)
- Majoo: a general POS for F&B, retail and salons, with "40,000+ merchants in 600+ cities". It has no stated pet or boarding module. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1642259472)
- HashMicro's petshop POS roundup (vendor content) lists POS apps for recording product sales, grooming services and non-cash payments. — [HashMicro blog](https://www.hashmicro.com/id/blog/kasir-petshop/) (403 on fetch; snippet only)
- Global benchmark: MoeGo (US) covers grooming, boarding and daycare scheduling and payments. I found no Indonesian equivalent. — [App Store MoeGo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1561621817)

### Inferences
- The likely "current stack" is WhatsApp Business (booking, photo and video updates) + Instagram (discovery) + a notebook or Google Sheets (cage board) + optionally a generic POS for the retail shelf + bank transfer or QRIS. Any SaaS must integrate with WhatsApp rather than replace it.
- The documented pains are: (1) sending daily updates to many owners, (2) manual records causing double-bookings and lost revenue, and (3) peak-season capacity management.

### Gaps
- No survey quantifying tool usage (for example, the share of operators using a POS or spreadsheets). Primary interviews are needed.
- I could not confirm whether Moka, Olsera or Pawoon offer boarding or reservation modules.

## 5. Aggregators and marketplaces for pet hotels in Indonesia

### Takeaway
I found no dominant pet hotel aggregator in Indonesia. Petloka (2019) launched a pet hotel search with 40 partners, and its current status is unknown. Most other examples are student or academic prototypes (Paws Inn, Petto, Mercu Buana). Discovery happens through Google Maps, Instagram and listicle articles, which leaves the space open.

### Cited Findings
- Petloka has a marketplace plus a pet hotel search feature (web and Android) with 40 verified partners in Jabodetabek, Surabaya, Yogyakarta and Bali. Its revenue model and commission were not disclosed (May 2019). — [detikInet](https://inet.detik.com/cyberlife/d-4559318/aplikasi-ini-bisa-bantu-cari-hotel-untuk-hewan-peliharaan)
- Academic prototypes include Paws Inn (UMN, dog boarding search), a Mercu Buana web app for finding the nearest boarding in Jakarta Barat, and Petto (Binus, vet booking). — [UMN](https://kc.umn.ac.id/id/eprint/34415/); [Mercu Buana](https://repository.mercubuana.ac.id/76414); [Binus Petto](https://binus.ac.id/gallery/product/528/petto/)
- Consumer discovery runs through editorial listicles of pet hotel recommendations, for example Popbela, Medcom and Hello Sehat. — [Popbela](https://www.popbela.com/lifestyle/travel/7-rekomendasi-pet-hotel-di-jakarta-tempat-inap-anabul-selama-mudik-00-m9g5t-hrtywy); [Medcom](https://www.medcom.id/gaya/wisata/Rb1y37Xb-ini-4-rekomendasi-pet-hotel-di-jakarta-buat-anabul-yang-butuh-healing-juga); [Hello Sehat](https://hellosehat.com/sehat/informasi-kesehatan/pet-hotel-jakarta/)
- Adjacent funded players focus on food and vet care, not boarding: Compawnion (East Ventures backed; Pawmeals, UGO) and Medivet (3 outlets). Compawnion now partners with listed distributor MDLA. — [OCBC Ventura](https://ocbcnispventura.substack.com/p/the-paw-sitive-trend-of-furry-friends); [Kontan](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/gandeng-compawnion-medela-potentia-mdla-perkuat-distribusi-segmen-pet-care)

### Inferences
- With no booking marketplace, a SaaS can later add a consumer booking or discovery layer, a "B2B first, then B2B2C" path.
- Repeated student projects on the same problem point to a recognized need that no commercial product has taken over.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether Petloka is still operating, or whether newer players exist (for example pet features in super-apps, or local "Pawpaw"/"PetPals"-type apps). Searches surfaced none. An app-store search for "pet hotel" / "penitipan hewan" would confirm this.
- I found no data on marketplace take rates or on consumers' willingness to book pet hotels online.
